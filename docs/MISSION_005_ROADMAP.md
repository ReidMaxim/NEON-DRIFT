# Mission 005 — evidence-led next steps

1. Refine the remaining dark peripheral recesses and sparse distant facades identified by independent review. Preserve foreground hierarchy and landmark silhouettes instead of adding uniform microdetail.
2. Add a few more structurally different modules per family, especially lower service fronts and corner buildings. Keep site/host constraints and test all facade orientations; avoid another layer of loose fixtures.
3. Expand the repeatable tours to integrated GPUs and another browser engine before enlarging the map. Retain CPU submission, GPU queries, initialization and resource measurements; synchronized frame intervals do not prove headroom.
4. Investigate atlas-based ordinary frontage text and batch fragmentation only where measured submission cost supports it. Keep the current engine and bounded resource design.
5. Improve side-of-road boundary readability in parcel gaps without creating fake accessible alleys. New traversable spaces require navigation, collision and source-height support together.
6. If vertical exploration is later desired, first design explicit ground height, stair/step response, supported landings and fall boundaries. Do not turn the current scenery into implied routes by adding decorative stairs.
7. Preserve the one-click preview and automatically verified publication bundle. Prefer CI artifacts for large future raw capture matrices so the repository does not grow indefinitely with evidence images.

Keep combat, complex NPC simulation, multiplayer and inventory expansion out of scope until environmental and current puzzle acceptance supports a new direction. Continue to fix generator rules across seeds rather than adjusting individual screenshots.
